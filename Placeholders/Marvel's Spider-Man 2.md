# Placeholders
|Placeholder|Function|
|--|--|
|{level}|Shows your current level|
|{ultimatelevel}|Shows your Ultimate Level in New Game Plus|
|{newgameplus}|Shows New Game or New Game Plus|
|{character}|Shows the name of the current playing character|
|{characterid}|Shows the current character ID|
|{character_icon}|Icon name of the current character, to be used with Large-/Smallasset|
|{location}|Shows your current location|
|{locationid}|Shows the current location ID|
|{health}|Shows your current health|
|{maxhealth}|Shows your maximum health|
|{skillpoints}|Shows your available skill points|
|{experience}|Shows your current level experience|
|{experience_total}|Shows your total experience|
|{experience_required}|Shows the experience required for the next level|
|{difficulty}|Shows your current difficulty|
|{difficultyid}|Shows the current difficulty ID|
