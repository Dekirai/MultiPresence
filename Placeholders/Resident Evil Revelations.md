# Placeholders
|Placeholder|Function|Usable in|
|--|--|--|
|{character}|Shows your current character|Campaign, Lobby, Ingame|
|{partner}|Shows your partner's name|Campaign, Ingame|
|{partnerdisplay}|Adds the partner's name with a leading separator|Campaign, Ingame|
|{episode}|Shows the current episode number|Campaign|
|{episodetitle}|Shows the title of the current episode|Campaign|
|{chapter}|Shows the episode number and title|Campaign|
|{difficulty}|Shows the campaign or Raid Mode difficulty|Campaign, Ingame|
|{difficultydisplay}|Adds the difficulty with a leading separator|Campaign, Ingame|
|{location}|Shows your current location|Campaign, Ingame|
|{locationdisplay}|Adds the current location with a leading separator|Campaign, Ingame|
|{objective}|Shows the current objective|Campaign|
|{stage}|Shows the Raid Mode stage number and name|Ingame|
|{stagenumber}|Shows the Raid Mode stage number|Ingame|
|{stagename}|Shows the Raid Mode stage name|Ingame|
|{level}|Shows the level of your Raid Mode character|Lobby, Ingame|
|{leveldisplay}|Adds the character level in brackets|Lobby, Ingame|
