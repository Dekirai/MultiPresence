# Placeholders
|Placeholder|Function|Usable in|
|--|--|--|
|{character}|Shows the party leader you are controlling|Ingame|
|{party}|Shows the other party members|Ingame|
|{partydisplay}|Adds the other party members with a leading separator|Ingame|
|{level}|Shows the level of the party leader|Ingame|
|{nextlevel}|Shows the EXP needed for the next level|Ingame|
|{hp}|Shows the current HP of the party leader|Ingame|
|{hpmax}|Shows the max HP of the party leader|Ingame|
|{mp}|Shows the current MP of the party leader|Ingame|
|{mpmax}|Shows the max MP of the party leader|Ingame|
|{lb}|Shows the Limit Burst gauge of the party leader in percent|Ingame|
|{gil}|Shows your gil|Ingame|
|{difficulty}|Shows the current difficulty|Ingame|
|{chapter}|Shows the chapter number and title|Ingame|
|{chaptertitle}|Shows the title of the current chapter|Ingame|
|{objective}|Shows the current main story objective|Ingame|
|{location}|Shows your current location|Ingame|
