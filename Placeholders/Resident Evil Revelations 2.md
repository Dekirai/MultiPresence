# Placeholders
|Placeholder|Function|Usable in|
|--|--|--|
|{character}|Shows your current character|Campaign, Lobby, Ingame|
|{campaign}|Shows the main campaign or extra episode name|Campaign|
|{campaigndisplay}|Shows the main episode number or extra episode title|Campaign|
|{episode}|Shows the episode number in the main campaign|Campaign|
|{location}|Shows your current location|Campaign|
|{locationdisplay}|Adds the current location with a leading separator|Campaign|
|{partner}|Shows your partner's name|Campaign|
|{partnerdisplay}|Adds the partner's name with a leading separator|Campaign|
|{objective}|Shows the current objective|Campaign|
|{level}|Shows the level of your character|Lobby, Ingame|
|{money}|Shows the current amount of money|Lobby, Ingame|
|{playername}|Shows your player name|Lobby, Ingame|
|{redhearts}|Shows your red Life Crystals|Lobby, Ingame|
|{bluehearts}|Shows your blue Life Crystals|Lobby, Ingame|
|{medallions}|Shows your total Raid Medallions|Lobby, Ingame|
|{chapter}|Shows the current chapter|Ingame|
|{mission}|Shows the current mission|Ingame|
|{missiondisplay}|Shows the mission number, difficulty and recommended level|Ingame|
|{difficulty}|Shows the mission difficulty|Ingame|
|{recommendedlevel}|Shows the recommended character level|Ingame|
|{kills}|Shows the number of enemies defeated|Ingame|
|{enemytarget}|Shows the target number of enemies|Ingame|
|{enemyprogress}|Adds the enemy counter with a leading separator|Ingame|
