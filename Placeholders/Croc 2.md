# Placeholders
|Placeholder|Function|
|--|--|
|{health}|Shows your current health|
|{maxhealth}|Shows your maximum health|
|{bonushearts}|Shows your bonus hearts|
|{crystals}|Shows the crystals on your Reward Card|
|{currentcrystals}|Shows level crystals in a level and Reward Card crystals in the overworld or shop|
|{levelcrystals}|Shows the crystals collected in the current level|
|{goldengobbos}|Shows your collected Golden Gobbos|
|{keys}|Shows your keys|
|{clockworkgobbos}|Shows your Clockwork Gobbos|
|{location}|Shows your current location|
|{world}|Shows your current world|
|{level}|Shows your current level name|
|{mapid}|Shows the current map ID|
|{areatype}|Shows the current area type|
|{gameplaymode}|Shows your current gameplay mode|
|{isoverworld}|Shows whether you are in the overworld|
