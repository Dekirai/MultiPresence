# Placeholders
|Placeholder|Function|
|--|--|
|{level}|Shows the current level|
|{levelid}|Shows the current level ID|
|{map}|Shows the current level|
|{mapid}|Shows the current map ID|
|{world}|Shows the current dimension|
|{worldtime}|Shows the time period of the current dimension|
|{location}|Shows the current dimension and level|
|{character}|Shows the current character|
|{mode}|Shows Modern or Retro mode|
|{currentcrates}|Shows the current amount of destroyed crates|
|{maxcrates}|Shows the amount of destroyable crates|
|{deaths}|Shows your amount of deaths in the current level|
|{wumpas}|Shows your wumpa amount for the current mode|
|{wumpasretro}|Shows your wumpa amount in retro mode|
|{wumpasmodern}|Shows your wumpa amount in modern mode|
|{lives}|Shows your remaining lives in Retro mode; empty in Modern mode|
