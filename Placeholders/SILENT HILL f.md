# Placeholders
|Placeholder|Function|Usable in|
|--|--|--|
|{location}|Shows the current location|Ingame|
|{areaid}|Shows the current area ID|Ingame|
|{objective}|Shows the current objective with its progress|Ingame|
|{objectivename}|Shows the current objective without its counter|Ingame|
|{objectivecount}|Shows the current objective count, when available|Ingame|
|{objectivemaxcount}|Shows the required objective count, when available|Ingame|
|{health}|Shows your current health|Ingame|
|{maxhealth}|Shows your maximum health|Ingame|
|{healthpercentage}|Shows your current health as a percentage|Ingame|
|{stamina}|Shows your current stamina|Ingame|
|{maxstamina}|Shows your maximum stamina|Ingame|
|{staminapercentage}|Shows your current stamina as a percentage|Ingame|
