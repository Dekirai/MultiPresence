# Placeholders
|Placeholder|Function|
|--|--|
|{floor}|Shows the name of the current floor|
|{floortype}|Shows the name of the current floor type|
|{floortypeasset}|Icon name of the current floor type, to be used with Large/Smallasset|
|{time}|Shows the current time of your run as hh:mm:ss|
|{score}|Shows the current score in your run|
|{character}|Shows the name of the character you are playing|
|{characterid}|Shows the ID of the current character|
|{floornumber}|Shows the ID of the current floor|
|{stagetype}|Shows the ID of the current floor variant|
|{difficulty}|Shows the difficulty ID: 0 Normal, 1 Hard, 2 Greed, 3 Greedier|
|{characterasset}|Icon name of the current character, to be used with Large/Smallasset|
|{keys}|Shows the amount of keys you have|
|{bombs}|Shows the amount of bombs you have|
|{coins}|Shows the amount of coins you have|
|{maxredhearts}|Shows your maximum red hearts in half-heart units|
|{maxreadhearts}|Shows your maximum red hearts in half-heart units|
|{redhearts}|Shows your current red hearts in half-heart units|
|{whitehearts}|Shows the amount of white hearts you have|
|{bluehearts}|Shows your current soul hearts in half-heart units|
|{blackheartsmask}|Shows your black-heart bitmask|
|{bonehearts}|Shows the amount of bone hearts you have|
|{boneheartsmask}|Shows your bone-heart bitmask|
|{rottenhearts}|Shows the amount of rotten hearts you have|
|{goldenhearts}|Shows the amount of golden hearts you have|
|{winstreak}|Shows your current win streak|
|{edentokens}|Shows the amount of Eden tokens you have|
