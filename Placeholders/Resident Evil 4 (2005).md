# Placeholders

|Placeholder|Function|Usable in|
|--|--|--|
|{state}|Shows the configuration section: Main, Mercenaries, Assignment_Ada, Separate_Ways, or Title_Screen|Everywhere|
|{mode}|Shows the current game mode|Gameplay|
|{gamemode}|Shows the current game mode|Gameplay|
|{character}|Shows the name of the character you are playing|Gameplay|
|{characterid}|Shows the numeric ID of the current character|Gameplay|
|{difficulty}|Shows the current difficulty, when available|Gameplay|
|{difficultyid}|Shows the numeric ID of the current difficulty, when available|Gameplay|
|{health}|Shows your current health|Gameplay|
|{maxhealth}|Shows your current maximum health|Gameplay|
|{healthstatus}|Shows your current health status|Gameplay|
|{healthpercentage}|Shows your current health as a percentage value|Gameplay|
|{healthstatusemoji}|Shows your current health status as an emoji|Gameplay|
|{location}|Shows the current area and room|Gameplay|
|{area}|Shows the current area, or Main Menu on the title screen|Everywhere|
|{region}|Shows the current area|Gameplay|
|{room}|Shows the current room|Gameplay|
|{roomid}|Shows the numeric ID of the current room|Gameplay|
|{stageid}|Shows the numeric ID of the current stage|Gameplay|
|{area_icon_name}|Shows the area icon name for LargeAsset or SmallAsset|Gameplay|
|{chapter}|Shows the current chapter|Main & Separate Ways|
|{chapterid}|Shows the numeric ID of the current chapter, when available|Main & Separate Ways|
|{weapon}|Shows your equipped weapon, when available|Gameplay|
|{currentweapon}|Shows your equipped weapon, when available|Gameplay|
|{weaponid}|Shows the numeric item ID of your equipped weapon, when available|Gameplay|
|{ammo}|Shows the ammo of your equipped weapon, or ∞ for infinite ammo|Gameplay|
|{loadedammo}|Shows the numeric loaded ammo of your equipped weapon, when available|Gameplay|
|{reserveammo}|Shows reserve ammo for your equipped weapon, or ∞ for infinite ammo|Gameplay|
|{infiniteammo}|Shows whether your equipped weapon has infinite ammo, when available|Gameplay|
|{money}|Shows your current pesetas, when available|Main & Separate Ways|
|{score}|Shows your current score|Mercenaries|
