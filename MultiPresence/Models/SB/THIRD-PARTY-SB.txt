Stellar Blade location data
==========================

The short world identifiers and English region names in Locations.cs were
obtained from the user's installed Stellar Blade game data. Game content and
localized text remain the property of SHIFT UP and their respective owners.
This file does not place the game's data or localization under an open-source
license.

WorldTable binds the UWorld asset path to the game's world alias.
WorldTransitionTable and BossChallengeTable provide explicit localization keys.
For remaining worlds, ZoneEnvSpawnTable -> ZoneTable and ZoneCampTable provide
an explicit region relationship. English strings are from the shipped
SB/Content/Localization/Game/en/Game.archive.
SB-provenance.json records the joins, example rows, resource hashes, and tools.
These mappings describe regions, not nearest camps or exact room boundaries.
Unknown maps retain their internal names rather than receiving a guessed label.
